Agent display mockups

Ten ways to show what agents exist, what they are doing, who they spawned, and what they need from you.

Every mockup renders the same simulated field of agents at three scales and shares one thread drawer. The first ones keep today's layout and use only signals the checkpoint already carries. The later ones assume richer checkpoints: a stable identity, a role, structured asks, a plan, live activity, and dependency edges. Open a mockup, press Play to let the simulation run, answer an ask, and click any agent to open its thread.

Keyboard inside a mockup: [ previous · ] next · . step the simulation · Esc close the drawer
